About Me

I am Raghav Rathore, an aspiring philosopher and independent thinker interested in Indian philosophy, critical thinking, self-knowledge, logic, and evidence-based inquiry.

My interests include questions about truth, consciousness, religion, society, freedom of thought, and the meaning of human life. Through reading, writing, and sharing ideas, I aim to learn continuously and encourage independent thinking.

“The aim is not to claim every answer, but to ask better questions and examine ideas honestly.”
